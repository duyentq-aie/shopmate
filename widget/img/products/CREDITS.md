# Photo credits (demo page)

The product photos of the demo shop page (`widget/demo.html`) and its hero picture. All of them are free-licence stock photos from the two sources the
project allows, **Unsplash** (Unsplash License) and **Pexels** (Pexels License). Both licences allow use, copying and modification, with or without
credit; the credit is given here because the photographers deserve it. No photo was altered beyond what the last line says.

Every photo was **downloaded** (nothing is hot-linked) on **2026-10-12**, cropped to 4:5, scaled to 600 px wide and saved as WebP, at most 120 KB each
(`<SKU>.webp` in this folder). The hero is cropped to 3:2, 960 px wide (`../hero.webp`). Pictures are illustrations: the shop, its products and its
prices are fictional (see the README), so a photo shows the right kind of product in the right colour, not the product itself. No photo shows a person
or a readable brand name.
